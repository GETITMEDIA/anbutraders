OUR SIX PILLARS OF TRUST

Uncompromising Structural Integrity

Every order fulfilled by Anbu Traders is backed by transparent verification and engineering standards.

100% Certified BIS Mill Material

We stock only primary and authorized mill materials with zero compromise. Mill test certificates from Tata Tiscon, JSW, UltraTech, and Dalmia are provided for every batch.

  • Direct factory consignment with batch traceability
  • Fe 500D, Fe 550D & CRS standard compliant
  • Guaranteed zero re-rolled or substandard steel

100-Ton Digital Weighbridge

Our facility operates an advanced computerized electronic weighbridge. Every dispatch receives a computer-printed weight ticket with zero calculation discrepancy.

  • Tare & gross weight verification on every delivery
  • Government weights & measures certified calibration
  • Complete billing transparency with no hidden short-weight

Direct Factory Wholesale Rates

As direct first-tier authorized distributors, we procure in high-volume railway rakes and pass substantial pricing advantages directly to our customers.

  • Eliminates intermediary agent commissions
  • Tiered discounts for large-scale builder projects
  • Real-time daily market rate transparency

Fleet Logistics & Crane Unloading

Our dedicated transport fleet includes heavy articulated lorries and truck-mounted cranes to deliver directly into difficult site bays with minimum turnaround.

  • Same-day and zero-hour site dispatch
  • Mechanical crane offloading prevents material bending
  • Seamless delivery across Pondicherry, Cuddalore & TN

Computerized Color Showroom

Our flagship Anbu Paints division offers instantaneous computerized shade dispensing for over 2,000+ interior, exterior, and protective industrial coatings.

  • Authorized Asian Paints, JSW Paints & Berger center
  • Spectrophotometer digital color matching
  • Waterproofing and masonry chemical experts

Consolidated Structural Supply

Save procurement management time by sourcing everything from foundational rebars and cements to roofing sheets, pipes, and finishing coats under one trusted invoice.

  • 3,000+ SKUs in continuous warehouse stock

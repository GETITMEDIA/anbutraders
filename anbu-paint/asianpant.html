PROFESSIONAL WORKMANSHIP

The Master Asian Paints Royale System

Follow Asian Paints' recommended multi-layer system to guarantee Teflon stain washability and maximum sheen longevity.

01

TruCare Primer Coat

Apply 1 coat of TruCare Interior Wall Primer diluted with water. Fills microscopic plaster cavities and locks in loose chalking particles.

02

2 Coats Wall Putty

Apply 2 coats of Birla White Putty using a steel blade. Sand gently with 180-grit paper, then finish with 320-grit for an ultra-flat satin foundation.

03

2 Coats Royale Glitz

Dilute 1 liter Royale with 400ml clean water. Apply 2 coats with a lambwool roller with 4 to 6 hours drying time between coats for luxurious reflection.

MASTER APPLICATION PROCESS

How to Apply Birla Wall Putty Properly

Follow these 3 professional steps for mirror-smooth surfaces and maximum paint adhesion.

01

Surface Pre-Wetting

Clean dust, grease, and efflorescence. Thoroughly pre-wet the plastered wall with clean water before application to prevent premature drying.

02

First Coat (Bottom to Top)

Mix 38-40% clean water to putty powder. Apply 1st coat uniformly using a putty blade from bottom to top (approx 1.5mm thickness). Allow 4 hours drying.

03

Second Coat & Fine Sanding

Apply 2nd thin coat (approx 1.0mm). Let it cure for 24 hours, then gently sand with 320 or 500-grit waterproof emery paper for a mirror-like finish.

About Our Wall Putty

Our premium wall putty is specially formulated to deliver a superior smooth finish, exceptional bonding strength, and long-lasting durability. It prepares your walls perfectly for painting by filling uneven surfaces, preventing flakiness, and enhancing overall paint life. Suitable for both interior and exterior surfaces, our wall putty ensures a flawless, crack-free, moisture-resistant base for all types of paint applications.

Applications

  • Levelling and smoothing interior walls before painting
  • Providing a uniform base on cement plastered surfaces
  • Filling minor dents, cracks, and surface imperfections
  • Enhancing paint adhesion on both interior and exterior walls
  • Protecting walls from moisture and dampness

Benefits of Our Wall Putty

  • Provides a smooth, premium finish to all types of walls
  • Strong adhesion ensures long-lasting paint durability
  • Prevents flaking, peeling, and cracking of paint
  • Moisture-resistant formula protects walls from dampness
  • Suitable for both interior and exterior wall surfaces
  • Improves overall paint coverage and reduces paint consumption

About Us

About Anbu Traders

Started in 2014 as a proprietary company with the name of Anbu Traders, the company slowly and diligently built up a repertoire of blue-chip clients. “Our service and product quality standards have won us accolades from various esteemed customers, including major infrastructure companies, builders, and architects.” We at Anbu Traders believe in building relationships and the testimony to this is that our customers continue to remain with us. We are one of the best dealers of steel and cement suppliers in Pondicherry.

We have a dedicated team working round the clock tirelessly to deliver materials on time. With our comprehensive facilities, we meet our customers’ demands with zero-hour precision. We offer wholesale supplies of steel and cement and are committed to extending our services further. Our infrastructure is equipped to handle large-scale wholesale orders and meet all our clients' needs.
